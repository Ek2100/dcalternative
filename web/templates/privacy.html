What we store, and how deletion works

When you verify

  • Never stored: your IP address, location, internet provider, email or full browser “user agent”. Your IP is used for a few milliseconds to check for VPNs (using a database on our own server, not a third party) and by Cloudflare's captcha, then thrown away.
  • Stored, per server: your Discord ID (encrypted), whether you're verified, and codes made from browser details: screen size, time zone, languages, graphics card, fonts, how your browser draws a test image and plays a test sound, a coarse browser family, and a random ID we keep in this browser. The codes are keyed hashes: they can't be turned back into the details.
  • Each server has its own key, so the same browser gives different codes in different servers. Nobody, including us, can link your accounts across servers. We will never offer a “look someone up” feature.

Who sees what

If your browser matches an account this server banned, its moderators are told which banned account you matched. You are only told that a moderator will review your verification. The bot cannot ban anyone. Moderators decide.

Deletion

  • You can delete your data any time with /privacy in Discord. Your verified role is removed straight away and your record stops being used. The rest is erased within 7 days.
  • If a server bans the account during those 7 days, they keep a ban record. Otherwise someone could break the rules, delete their data and come straight back on a new account.
  • Ban records can't be deleted by the banned person. They're deleted after the period the server chose (30–365 days, default 180), or right away if the person is unbanned.
  • Verified members' records are deleted 180 days after verifying, or 30 days after leaving the server.
  • When a server removes the bot or presses “Delete all server data”, we destroy that server's encryption key first, which makes all its records unreadable, and then delete the records.
  • Unused verification links expire after 10 minutes.
