Before you verify

To stop banned people coming back on new accounts, this server checks whether your browser looks like one used by another account in this server.

  • We read some technical details of your browser (like screen size, graphics card and fonts) and keep a random ID in this browser. We turn them into codes that can't be turned back, and keep only the codes.
  • We check whether you're on a VPN, but we never store your IP address.
  • Nothing is shared with other servers. Records are deleted automatically, and you can delete yours any time with /privacy in Discord.
  • A quick Cloudflare check makes sure you're human.
